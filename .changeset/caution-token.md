New `--vx-caution` colour (#f2994a, amber between `--vx-warn` and `--vx-bad`) for "worse than degraded, not yet down". `Health` gains `'caution'`, so `VxUptimeBar` ticks, `VxStatusDot` and `VxChip tone="caution"` use it. The Tokens story lists every colour token with its value and meaning.
